# Instructions

These guide Claude when it writes {personal_line} and {blurb}. Everything else in the
subject and body above is filled in mechanically and sent exactly as written.

- personal_line: one sentence to {first_name} that shows why I'm writing to them in
  particular: something specific about their team or path. Don't introduce me or say
  I'm a {school_short} student (the next paragraph does that). Mention only connections
  that are actually true (we went to the same school). No flattery, no "I hope this
  finds you well".
- blurb: 2–3 sentences in the third person ("{my_first_name} is…") that a referrer could
  paste into a referral form. Use only facts from my resume that matter for this job.
  No made-up numbers or claims. Don't say what year I'm in (sophomore/junior); use
  "class of {class_year}".
- Keep it plain, friendly and brief. No exclamation marks in the blurb.
